# Anatomy of near-positive discordant clonotypes

Single-residue CDR3 variants with opposite functional labels, compared
against same-label (positive/positive) neighbour pairs at comparable
distance so that general properties of CDR3 mutation are not mistaken for
properties of discordance.

- discordant pairs: 71
- control pairs: 522
