2 · Not binding prediction — annotation reliabilitycore · 45 s

不是结合预测,是标注可信度

On screen: three white cards, then a teal band with the metric.
English

It is worth being precise about the task, because it is easy to hear it as specificity prediction. Every one of the 2,636 paired alpha-beta TCRs in test.csv is already reported in VDJdb as recognising its annotated peptide-HLA. We are not asked whether it binds.

We are asked whether that published claim reproduces. Re-tested under one standardised functional screen, roughly 44% of previously reported pairs showed no reactivity. So this is a database-auditing problem.

And the public half carries published labels, so the public leaderboard is a lookup — nineteen teams reached 0.984 or above. Only the 602 private rows, over three peptides never released, decide anything.

中文

任务要说准,因为它很容易被听成特异性预测。test.csv 里 2,636 条配对 αβ TCR,每一条都已经被 VDJdb 标注为识别某个肽-HLA。我们不是在判断它结不结合。

我们是在问:这条已发表的声称,重做一遍还成立吗。在统一条件的功能筛选下重测,大约 44% 的既往报告没有反应。所以这是一个数据库审计问题。

而且公开那一半的标签已经发表,所以公榜只是一次查表——十九支队伍达到 0.984 以上。真正算分的只有 602 行私榜数据,落在三个从未发布的肽上。

If asked about the metric: mean per-epitope ROC-AUC with ambiguous rows dropped. Only the ranking inside an epitope is read, and each epitope carries equal weight, so a 130-row cohort counts as much as a 243-row one.
指标: 表位内 ROC-AUC 再按表位等权平均,ambiguous 行剔除。只读表位内部的排序,130 行的队列和 243 行的队列权重相同。

3 · Real binders converge; artefacts are random drawscore · 70 s

真结合者趋同,假配对是随机抽取

On screen: five histograms, one per public peptide, sorted by AUC. GLCTLVAML has an orange background.
English

Our premise is one line of textbook immunology: T cells that recognise the same peptide-MHC converge on similar receptors — similar V-gene usage, similar CDR3 motifs.

Turn that around. A TCR that entered the database through a technical artefact — tetramer background, barcode crosstalk, a mispaired alpha and beta chain from a single-cell experiment — is effectively a random draw from the repertoire. It has no reason to resemble anything else in its own cohort.

So: the real ones have company, the false ones sit alone. This figure is that sentence. The x-axis is the distance from each TCR to its three nearest neighbours of the same peptide. Teal is validated, orange is not. Four of the five separate cleanly — validated pairs pile up on the left in crowded neighbourhoods, unvalidated pairs on the right, isolated.

Look at the right-hand panel. GLCTLVAML, AUC 0.677: the two distributions sit on top of each other. The same measure reaches 0.927 on YLQPRTFLL. I will come back to that too.

中文

我们的前提是免疫学里一句老话:识别同一个肽-MHC 的 T 细胞,受体会趋同——V 基因用得像,CDR3 带相似基序。

反过来看。一条因技术假象进入数据库的 TCR——四聚体背景、条码串扰、单细胞实验里配错的 α/β 链——本质上是从整个受体库里随机掉出来的一条。它没有理由长得像自己队列里的任何人。

所以:真的有同行,假的落单。这张图就是这句话。横轴是每条 TCR 到同肽最近三个邻居的距离,蓝绿是验证通过的,橙色是没通过的。五个里有四个分得很干净——验证过的堆在左边、邻域拥挤,没验证的堆在右边、孤零零。

看最右边那格。GLCTLVAML,AUC 0.677:两条分布几乎完全叠在一起。同一个量在 YLQPRTFLL 上能到 0.927。这一点我待会儿也会回来。

How to read it / 怎么读: each panel is one public peptide. Bars are densities, so the two colours each integrate to 1 — the overlap area is the error the measure would make. Panels are sorted left to right by AUC, so the story reads itself. The small grey text gives the positive/negative counts.
每格是一个公开肽。条形是密度,两种颜色各自积分为 1——重叠面积就是这个量会犯的错。各格按 AUC 从高到低排,所以故事自己读得出来。灰色小字是正/负样本数。

5 · The relation is smooth — there is no threshold to findoptional · 40 s

关系是平滑的,找不到阈值

On screen: validation rate against distance, one line per peptide, plus a thick black pooled line.
English

This is the slide for anyone who asks why there is no clustering step. The black line pools all five public peptides. It slides from 0.9 down to 0.1 with no step anywhere: the largest single drop is −0.216 against a mean step of 0.107, a ratio of only 2.0. A real threshold would show one step far larger than the rest.

So "clustered versus singleton" is a lossy binary summary of a continuous quantity. That is why the submitted model uses k-nearest-neighbour distance and kernel density, and no clustering at all. Clustering appears only in the analysis layer, as single-linkage connected components at cosine distance 0.12.

中文

这页是留给"为什么不做聚类"这个问题的。黑色粗线是五个公开肽合并。它从 0.9 一路滑到 0.1,任何一处都没有台阶:最大单步 −0.216,平均步长 0.107,比值只有 2.0。真有阈值的话,会有一步远远甩开其他。

所以"成簇 vs 孤立"是一个连续量的有损二值摘要。这就是提交的模型用 k 近邻距离和核密度、完全不用聚类的原因。聚类只出现在分析层,是余弦距离 0.12 上的单连接连通分量。

7 · One question asked four ways, then averagedcore · 60 s

同一个问题问四遍,然后取平均

On screen: four numbered cards with arrows between them, then a teal band.
English

Four components, all computed strictly within the peptide cohort.

One, multi-space density: an exponential kernel density in three distance spaces — two SCEPTR embeddings at bandwidth 0.1, and paired alpha-plus-beta tcrdist3 at 20. Self-distances excluded.

Two, pooled reference support: the mean distance to the eight nearest same-peptide receptors after adding paired human VDJdb records, so that a small cohort still has somewhere to look.

Three, whole V-domain similarity: a top-eight alignment over the complete variable domain rather than CDR3 alone, so CDR1, CDR2 and the framework count.

Four, structural confidence: the minimum TCR-peptide predicted aligned error from a Boltz-2 model of the complex. It is the only term that does not look at neighbours.

Each becomes a within-epitope percentile rank, and the four ranks are averaged with equal weights. Why not fit the weights? Because only five peptides are available to fit on and the score comes from three that are not. Any weighting that helps on the visible ones is a bet that they resemble the hidden ones. Equal weights make no bet — and leave nothing to overfit.

中文

四个分项,全部严格在表位队列内部计算。

一,多空间密度:三个距离空间上的指数核密度——两个 SCEPTR 嵌入,带宽 0.1;双链 α+β tcrdist3,带宽 20。排除自身距离。

二,参考池支持:加入配对人源 VDJdb 记录之后,到最近八条同肽受体的平均距离,好让小队列也有地方找近亲。

三,全 V 结构域相似性:在完整可变域而不是只在 CDR3 上做 top-8 比对,这样 CDR1、CDR2 和框架区也算数。

四,结构置信度:Boltz-2 建出的复合物里 TCR–肽最小预测对齐误差。它是唯一不看邻居的一项。

每一项转成表位内百分位名次,四个名次等权平均。为什么不去拟合权重?因为能用来拟合的只有五个肽,而计分的是另外三个。任何"在可见的那五个上更好"的权重,本质上是在赌它们像那三个。等权不下这个赌注——也因此没有东西可以过拟合。

8 · A density model with three correctionscore · 45 s

一个密度模型加三项修正

On screen: left, four horizontal bars with dots; right, a four-step ladder with an orange bracket.
English

Left panel: each of the four terms scored on its own. Bars are the macro average, dots are the five public peptides. The three distance measures land at 0.851, 0.843 and 0.832; the structural term is the weakest at 0.757 — and on TTDPSFLGRY it falls to 0.629.

Right panel: the ladder. A single SCEPTR density alone is 0.8396. Adding structure takes it to 0.8498, the V-domain to 0.8557, the pooled reference to 0.8600. So the first term alone is 0.3396 of the 0.3600 above chance — 94.3%, and the other three together buy +0.0204.

I want to be explicit about what that means: this is not a structure model with some density help. It is a density model, and structure is the weakest of its four terms.

中文

左图:四个分项各自单独评分。条形是宏平均,圆点是五个公开肽。三个距离度量落在 0.851、0.843、0.832;结构项最弱,0.757——在 TTDPSFLGRY 上更是只有 0.629。

右图:阶梯。单一 SCEPTR 密度自己是 0.8396。加上结构到 0.8498,加 V 结构域到 0.8557,加参考池到 0.8600。所以第一项自己就占了高于随机的 0.3600 里的 0.3396,即 94.3%,其余三项加起来只买到 +0.0204。

我想把这句话说明白:这不是一个带点密度辅助的结构模型。它是一个密度模型,而结构是四项里最弱的一项。

"Above chance" / 「高于随机」: an AUC of 0.5 is chance, so the usable range is 0.5 to 1.0. D reaches 0.8600, i.e. 0.3600 above chance; a single density reaches 0.8396, i.e. 0.3396. The ratio is 94.3%.
AUC 0.5 是随机,所以可用区间是 0.5 到 1.0。D 到 0.8600,即高于随机 0.3600;单一密度到 0.8396,即 0.3396。比值 94.3%。

9 · The simplest entry woncore · 50 s

最朴素的那一份赢了

On screen: left, a slope chart of our five entries; right, all 165 scored entries by rank.
English

Left: our five entries, public on the left of each line, private on the right. The ordering is close to reversed. Our consensus entry went 0.882 public to 0.811 private; the nine-refinement entry 0.871 to 0.810; and the plainest four-term average, 0.860 public, scored 0.819 — the best of all of them.

We quantified that. On a bootstrap of the public rows shaped like the private split, the consensus was expected to beat the plain average by +0.022 plus or minus 0.006. The realised margin was −0.008. That is about five paired standard deviations, so the relationship genuinely changed between the public and the private peptides.

Right: the whole board. Seven entries sit at 0.95 and above, then the field drops to 0.846 and declines smoothly to 0.467. We are the circled point. The shaded band is the gap two unrelated methods would need to be told apart at 95% confidence — 0.047 — and 35 of 107 teams sit inside it. The top third of the honest field is one large tie.

中文

左图:我们五份提交,每条线左端是公榜、右端是私榜。顺序几乎翻转了。共识那一份从公榜 0.882 掉到私榜 0.811;加了九项修正的从 0.871 到 0.810;而最朴素的四项等权平均,公榜 0.860,私榜 0.819——是全部里最好的。

这个我们量过。在一个按私榜形状构造的公开行自助重采样上,共识对朴素平均的预期优势是 +0.022 ± 0.006。实际是 −0.008。相当于约五个配对标准差,所以公开肽和私榜肽之间的关系确实变了。

右图:整个榜单。七份提交在 0.95 以上,然后掉到 0.846,再平滑下降到 0.467。圈出来的是我们。阴影带是两个不相关方法要在 95% 置信度下分出高下所需的差距——0.047——而107 支队伍里有 35 支落在里面。诚实组的前三分之一是一个大并列。

Note on the two rank numbers / 两个名次的说明: the circled point sits at position 34 on the x-axis, which counts submissions. "19th of 107" counts teams, after collapsing multi-account clusters and removing the seven label-recovery entries. Say one of them out loud, not both.
圈出的点在横轴第 34 位,那是按提交数的排名。"107 支队伍里第 19"是按队伍算的,已并掉多账号集群并移除七份查表提交。口头只说一个,不要两个都说。

10 · GLCTLVAML: the premise does not holdrecommended · 35 s

GLCTLVAML:前提不成立

On screen: the GLC score distribution, plus an orange box of text.
English

I would rather volunteer the failure than be asked about it. On GLCTLVAML the validated and non-validated receptors sit on top of each other. Density alone reaches 0.927 on YLQPRTFLL and 0.677 here.

And it is not a structural story either. Across six geometric spread measures this cohort ranks 4.3 of 6 — unexceptional — and its per-position contact footprint matches the other HLA-A*02:01 nonamers. Across roughly 150 experiments nothing recovered it. The method depends on within-cohort convergence; where there is none, there is nothing to measure.

中文

与其被问到,不如主动讲。在 GLCTLVAML 上,验证过和没验证过的受体完全叠在一起。单独密度在 YLQPRTFLL 上到 0.927,在这里只有 0.677。

而且这也不是一个结构故事。在六个几何展度量上,这个队列平均排 4.3/6——毫不特殊——它的逐位接触足迹和其他 HLA-A*02:01 九肽一致。大约 150 个实验里没有一个救回来。这个方法依赖队列内部的趋同;哪里没有趋同,哪里就没有东西可量。

11 · Five independent channels, one shapeoptional · 45 s

五条独立通道,同一个形状

On screen: dark slide, a five-row table, then a summary band.
English

This is the slide I would keep for a methodological audience. Five channels, five genuinely different knowledge sources: wet-lab negatives, atomic chemistry, docking quality, general protein statistics, and a composition null.

The middle column is the residual after the model is removed — that is, how much each channel still predicts once what the model already knows is taken out. The floor is 0.5098. All five land between the floor and 0.5609, and none of them converts to ranking gain.

HERMES is the cleanest of the five on provenance: it has never seen a TCR, a pMHC, or a binding label. That it also gives exactly +0.0000 is the strongest form the result can take.

中文

这页是给方法学听众留的。五条通道,五个真正不同的知识来源:湿实验阴性、原子化学、对接质量、通用蛋白统计、组成零假设。

中间那列是去掉模型之后的残差——也就是把模型已经知道的东西扣掉之后,每条通道还能预测多少。地板是 0.5098。五条全部落在地板和 0.5609 之间,而且没有一条转化成排序增益。

HERMES 在来源上是最干净的一条:它从没见过 TCR、没见过 pMHC、没见过任何结合标签。它同时给出恰好 +0.0000,是这个结论能有的最强形式。

HERMES 那一行用的是 hermes_bp_000(biopython 预处理)。论文自己的 benchmark 行用的是 hermes_py_000(pyrosetta),在一个 16 肽面板上两种配置的 Spearman 相差 0.44。所以严谨说法是"在 bp 配置下"。

模型与工具 / Models and tools

SCEPTR
Simple Contrastive Embedding of the Primary sequence of T-cell Receptors
TCR 一级序列的对比嵌入模型
A self-supervised TCR language model. Takes TRAV, CDR3α, TRAJ, TRBV, CDR3β and TRBJ, returns a 64-dimensional vector, and never sees a binding label. 自监督的 TCR 语言模型。输入这六个字段,输出 64 维向量,从未见过结合标签。
tcrdist3
tcrdist3
A hand-designed biochemical distance between TCRs: BLOSUM substitution costs weighted by CDR loop, plus gap penalties. 手工设计的 TCR 间生化距离:按 CDR 环加权的 BLOSUM 替换代价加 gap 惩罚。
Boltz-2
Boltz-2
An open structure-prediction model in the AlphaFold family, used here to fold all 2,636 complexes. AlphaFold 同类的开源结构预测模型,这里用它折叠全部 2,636 个复合物。
AF3 / AlphaFold3
AlphaFold3
DeepMind structure predictor; the organisers structural baseline used its minimum PAE. DeepMind 的结构预测器;主办方的结构基线用的是它的最小 PAE。
MSA
multiple sequence alignment
多序列比对
The evolutionary input most folding models use. Ours were run with EMPTY MSAs. 多数折叠模型使用的进化信息输入。我们是用空 MSA 跑的。
HERMES
HERMES
A rotationally equivariant network that predicts which amino acid belongs at a masked position from its local atomic neighbourhood. Pre-trained on general protein structures, never on binding data. 旋转等变网络,从局部原子邻域预测被遮住的位置该是哪种氨基酸。在通用蛋白结构上预训练,从未见过结合数据。
BLOSUM62
BLOcks SUbstitution Matrix
BLOSUM62 替换矩阵
The standard amino-acid substitution score table, and our sequence baseline. 标准的氨基酸替换打分表,也是我们的序列基线。
Needleman–Wunsch
Needleman–Wunsch 算法
Global sequence alignment, used for the whole-V-domain distance with BLOSUM62, gap open 11 and extend 1. 全局序列比对,用于全 V 结构域距离,配 BLOSUM62、gap open 11、extend 1。
OLGA / Pgen
generation probability of a receptor
受体生成概率
How likely a receptor is to arise from random V(D)J recombination. Tested and found unhelpful here. 一条受体由随机 V(D)J 重组产生的概率。测过,在这里没有帮助。
reduce
reduce
The program that places hydrogen atoms on a structure. HERMES bp models take hydrogens as a trained input channel, so it is part of the method, not a formality. 给结构加氢原子的程序。HERMES 的 bp 模型把氢当作训练输入通道,所以它是方法的一部分,不是形式。
biopython / PyRosetta parser
biopython / PyRosetta 解析器
The two structure-preprocessing paths HERMES ships. hermes_bp_* uses biopython, hermes_py_* uses PyRosetta, and they are SEPARATELY TRAINED weights, not one model with two front ends. HERMES 提供的两条结构预处理路径。两者是各自训练的权重,不是同一个模型的两个前端。

结构量 / Structural quantities

PAE
predicted aligned error
预测对齐误差
The folding model own estimate, in ångströms, of how wrong the relative placement of two parts of the structure is. Lower is more confident; we use the minimum over TCR–peptide residue pairs. 折叠模型自己给出的估计,单位埃,表示结构两部分相对位置可能错多少。越低越自信;我们取 TCR–肽残基对上的最小值。
ipTM / pTM
interface predicted TM-score / predicted TM-score
界面预测 TM 分数 / 预测 TM 分数
Global confidence scores from folding models; ipTM is restricted to the interface. 折叠模型的全局置信度分数;ipTM 限定在界面上。
pLDDT
predicted Local Distance Difference Test
预测的局部距离差异测试分
Per-residue structural confidence. 逐残基的结构置信度。
pDockQ / ipSAE
predicted DockQ / interface Score of Alignment Error
预测对接质量 / 界面比对误差分
Two published scores summarising how good a predicted interface looks. Both tested here; neither converted to gain. 两个已发表的、概括预测界面质量的分数。都测过,都没有转化成增益。
SASA
solvent-accessible surface area
溶剂可及表面积
How exposed an atom is. One of HERMES input channels, and a quantity that legitimately changes when the TCR is deleted. 一个原子有多暴露。它是 HERMES 的输入通道之一,也是删掉 TCR 后会合理改变的量。
BSA
buried surface area
埋藏表面积
How much surface two partners hide from solvent by touching. 两个伙伴通过接触从溶剂中藏起来的表面积。
contact fingerprint
接触指纹
A 60-value vector: 2 chains × 10 peptide positions × 3 descriptors, indexed BY PEPTIDE POSITION — which is what lets two TCRs be compared by WHERE they touch. 60 维向量:2 条链 × 10 个肽位 × 3 个描述符,按肽位索引——所以能比较两条 TCR 碰在哪里。
crossing angle
交叉角
The angle at which the TCR lies across the MHC groove. TCR 斜跨 MHC 凹槽的角度。

统计与验证 / Statistics and validation

ROC
receiver operating characteristic
受试者工作特征曲线
The curve of true-positive against false-positive rate as a threshold moves. 阈值移动时,真阳性率对假阳性率画出的曲线。
AUC
area under the ROC curve
ROC 曲线下面积
Probability that a randomly chosen positive ranks above a randomly chosen negative. 0.5 is chance, 1.0 is perfect. 随机抽一个正样本排在随机抽一个负样本之上的概率。0.5 是随机,1.0 是完美。
macro-AUC
macro-averaged AUC
宏平均 AUC
AUC computed separately inside each epitope, then averaged with equal weight per epitope. This is the competition metric. 在每个表位内部各算一个 AUC,再按表位等权平均。这就是竞赛指标。
Spearman ρ
Spearman rank correlation
Spearman 秩相关
Correlation of ranks rather than values; used for the affinity panels. 用名次而不是数值算的相关;用于亲和力面板。
Kd
dissociation constant
解离常数
A measured binding affinity. Lower Kd means tighter binding, so panels plot minus log10 of Kd. 实测的结合亲和力。Kd 越低结合越紧,所以面板画的是 −log10(Kd)。
LOEO
leave-one-epitope-out
留一表位交叉验证
Fit or tune on all epitopes but one, evaluate on the held-out epitope. The only honest split here: random splits leak, because near-identical clonotypes land on both sides. 除一个表位外全部用于拟合/调参,在留出的表位上评估。这里唯一诚实的切分方式:随机切分会泄漏,因为近乎相同的克隆型会同时落在两边。
nested LOEO
嵌套 LOEO
LOEO with the configuration choice made inside the loop, so the reported number pays for the selection. 把配置选择也放进循环内部的 LOEO,报出的数字因此付了选择的代价。
permutation null
置换零假设
Re-run the whole selection on shuffled labels to see how large a gain pure noise produces. Here that null has sd 0.0076 and a maximum of +0.0203. 在打乱的标签上把整套选择重跑一遍,看纯噪声能产生多大增益。这里零假设的 sd 是 0.0076,最大 +0.0203。
bootstrap
自助重采样
Resample rows with replacement many times to get an interval around a statistic. 对行做有放回重采样多次,给统计量算一个区间。
jackknife / leave-one-out
刀切法 / 留一
Drop each observation in turn; the spread of the result shows whether a single point drives it. 逐个删掉每个观测;结果的跨度显示是不是某一个点在驱动它。
sd / CI
standard deviation / confidence interval
标准差 / 置信区间
Spread, and the interval that would cover the truth in 95% of repetitions. 离散程度,以及在 95% 的重复中会覆盖真值的区间。
residual-on-D
对 D 的残差
Regress a candidate feature on the model own score, then ask how well the LEFTOVER predicts. It tests new information rather than re-encoded information. The floor here is 0.5098. 把候选特征对模型自己的分数做回归,再问剩下的部分能预测多少。它检验的是新信息,不是重新编码的旧信息。这里地板是 0.5098。
typicality gate
典型性门槛
A standing check that a new feature is not simply re-measuring how typical a receptor is. 一道常设检查,确认新特征不是在重新测量受体的典型程度。
k-NN
k-nearest neighbours
k 近邻
The k closest points to a query; our density terms use k = 8, or the nearest three. 距离查询点最近的 k 个点;我们的密度项用 k = 8 或最近三个。
kernel density / bandwidth
核密度 / 带宽
A smooth measure of how crowded a neighbourhood is; the bandwidth sets what counts as near. 对邻域拥挤程度的平滑度量;带宽决定多近算近。
percentile rank
百分位名次
A score position within its own epitope, from 0 to 1. Averaging ranks rather than raw scores makes incomparable quantities comparable. 一个分数在它自己表位内的位置,0 到 1。平均名次而不是原始分数,能让不可比的量变得可比。
TwoNN / LID
two-nearest-neighbour estimator of local intrinsic dimensionality
局部本征维度的两近邻估计
Whether a neighbourhood is a flat sheet or a thin thread — sparseness in a different sense. 一个邻域是摊开的一片还是挤成一条线——另一种意义上的稀疏。
nn3
nn3
Shorthand for the mean distance to the three nearest same-epitope neighbours; the x-axis of the main figure. 到同表位最近三个邻居的平均距离的简写;主图的横轴。
Levenshtein distance
edit distance
编辑距离
The number of single-character insertions, deletions or substitutions between two strings. 两个字符串之间单字符插入、删除、替换的次数。

预备问答 / Prepared answers

Q · Why not use TCR-BERT, TULIP, NetTCR or any trained binding predictor?

为什么不用 TCR-BERT、TULIP、NetTCR 这类训练好的结合预测器?

English

Because they are trained on VDJdb, and this task audits VDJdb — it is circular. The strongest public candidate we tested overlaps the test set 100% and carries 872 inverted labels: pairs it was trained to call positive that the functional screen calls negative. We used none of them.

中文

因为它们都在 VDJdb 上训练过,而这个任务是在审计 VDJdb——构成循环论证。我们测过最强的那个公开候选,与测试集重叠 100%,并带有 872 条反转标签:它被训练成判阳性、而功能筛选判阴性的配对。我们一个都没用。

Q · What rank did you get?

你们排第几?

English

Nineteenth of 107 teams, after collapsing multi-account clusters and removing the seven label-recovery entries. But I would rather give the honest version: the top 35 of those 107 are inside one statistically indistinguishable band. Two unrelated methods need a gap of 0.047 to be told apart at 95% confidence, and the distance from the top honest score, 0.846, to ours, 0.819, is 0.027.

中文

并掉多账号集群、去掉七份查表提交之后,是 107 支队伍里的第 19。但我更愿意给诚实的版本:那 107 支里的前 35 支处在同一个统计上不可区分的带子里。两个不相关的方法要在 95% 置信度下分出高下需要 0.047 的差距,而诚实组天花板 0.846 到我们 0.819 只差 0.027。

Q · Does structure help at all?

结构到底有没有用?

English

Yes, but it is the weakest of the four terms: 0.757 standalone, and only 0.629 on TTDPSFLGRY. Its value is that it is the only term that does not look at neighbours, so it is an independent check rather than a stronger signal.

We also showed that the confidence score does not encode peptide specificity. We re-folded 300 TCRs against anchor-matched decoy peptides — 900 extra folds. Target-only confidence predicts validation at 0.63 to 0.74; subtract the decoy, which cancels everything that is a property of the TCR alone, and every margin collapses to chance: +0.0061 against a decoy-choice noise of 0.036. An interface containing no TCR at all already scores 0.647.

What I would not claim: that structure is useless for TCR work in general. We ran one setup — Boltz-2, empty MSAs, a reduced class-I platform — and no AlphaFold3 control. IMMREP25, a genuinely different task, found structure was what distinguished the top methods. Our reading is that structure did not get weaker; neighbourhood density on a populated cohort is simply much stronger, and leaves structure only the residual.

中文

有,但它是四项里最弱的:单独 0.757,在 TTDPSFLGRY 上只有 0.629。它的价值在于它是唯一不看邻居的一项,所以提供的是独立旁证,不是更强的信号。

我们还证明了置信度分数不编码肽特异性。我们把 300 条 TCR 对锚定匹配的诱饵肽重折了一遍——额外 900 次折叠。只看目标肽的置信度预测验证在 0.63 到 0.74;减掉诱饵——减掉的正是只属于这条 TCR 自身的成分——每个 margin 都塌到随机:+0.0061,而换一组诱饵的噪声就有 0.036。一个里面根本没有 TCR 的界面,已经能拿到 0.647。

我不会声称的是:结构对 TCR 工作普遍无用。我们只跑了一个设置——Boltz-2、空 MSA、简化的 I 类平台——而且没做 AlphaFold3 对照。IMMREP25 是一个确实不同的任务,它发现结构正是区分顶尖方法的东西。我们的解读是:结构没有变弱,是populated 队列上的邻域密度实在太强,只给结构留下了残差。

Q · Why is there no clustering step?

为什么没有聚类这一步?

English

We tried it, but only in the analysis layer. The validation rate declines smoothly with distance, with no step anywhere: largest single drop −0.216 against a mean step of 0.107, a ratio of 2.0. So clustered-versus-singleton is a lossy binarisation of a continuous quantity, and the submitted model uses the continuous version.

For the record, the clustering we did use for analysis is single-linkage connected components on a cosine-distance graph thresholded at 0.12 — which empirically means a neighbour whose CDR3 differs by a median of one residue, with 98% of such pairs sharing CDR3β length.

中文

试过,但只用在分析层。验证率随距离平滑下降,任何一处都没有台阶:最大单步 −0.216,平均步长 0.107,比值 2.0。所以"成簇 vs 孤立"是连续量的有损二值化,提交的模型用的是连续版本。

补充一句:我们用于分析的聚类是余弦距离图在 0.12 处阈值化后的单连接连通分量——经验上它的含义是,近亲的 CDR3 中位只差一个残基,而且 98% 这样的配对 CDR3β 长度相同。

Q · How confident are you in 0.819?

0.819 这个数你们有多确定?

English

As an absolute number, not very: bootstrapping the rows of a private-shaped split gives any single team a standard deviation of about 0.017. But that wobble is common to everyone, since all teams are scored on the same 602 rows, so it does not reorder the board. What reorders it is the paired difference, and for two unrelated methods that has a standard deviation of 0.024.

Worth adding: our pre-registered private estimate, written into the submission description on 26 October, was 0.798 to 0.802. The realised value was 0.819 — conservative by about 0.02, in the right direction.

中文

作为一个绝对数字,不太确定:对一个私榜形状的切分做行自助重采样,任何一支队伍的标准差约 0.017。但这个抖动对所有人是共同的——大家都在同一批 602 行上评分——所以它不会打乱排名。打乱排名的是配对差,而两个不相关方法的配对差标准差是 0.024。

值得补一句:我们在 10 月 26 日写进提交说明的预登记私榜估计是 0.798–0.802。实际是 0.819——保守了约 0.02,方向是对的。
