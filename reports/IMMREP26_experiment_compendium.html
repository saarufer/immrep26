How to read the numbers

The competition metric is the unweighted mean of per-epitope ROC AUC, with ambiguous rows dropped — reverse-engineered in E1 and confirmed three times against the leaderboard. Unless stated, every figure is that quantity over the five public epitopes.

Organisers' tcrdist3 baseline0.8247
Organisers' AlphaFold3 min-PAE baseline0.787
Standard error of a five-epitope estimate≈ 0.011
Standard error on the three private epitopes≈ 0.023
Pre-registered inclusion threshold for a new component> +0.02

The dataset is 2,636 rows in test.csv: 2,034 public (of which 1,782 carry a usable label and 252 are ambiguous) and 602 private across three epitopes. Only the private 602 decide the final ranking.

The submitted model

submissions/density3_struct03.csv, built by scripts/18_final_submission.py: a rank average of three within-epitope kernel densities — SCEPTR default and small at cosine σ=0.1, tcrdist3 α+β at σ=20 — blended 0.7 / 0.3 with Boltz-2 TCR–peptide minimum PAE. Fully unsupervised, zero fitted parameters.

The honest private estimate

0.80 central, plausible range 0.67–0.91. Not the 0.846 that nested leave-one-epitope-out reports. E30 is the reason, and it is the most consequential result here.

E2

Can the public labels simply be recovered?

Foundation

GoalTest whether the public leaderboard measures skill at all.

MethodJoin TCRvdb to test.csv and score the recovered label.

Resulttcr_id is shared verbatim: 2,034/2,034 rows match exactly, no fuzzy matching needed. Submitting the recovered label scores 1.000 on every public epitope. Simulating 5% match failure still gives 0.9988.

SettledThe public leaderboard measures a merge, not skill — the 1.000 and 0.997 standings are reproducible in one line. Public standing was dropped from consideration entirely after this. Do the private labels leak too? No. TCRvdb's changelog lists exactly which epitopes each release contains: YLQPRTFLL+GLCTLVAML (2025-05-01), plus TTDPSFLGRY, GILGFVFTL and NLVPMVATV (2026-10-01). The private three have never been released and are absent from the downloaded file. The private leaderboard is sound, so modelling decides the ranking.
E3

Can annotation provenance rank the private epitopes at all?

Foundation

GoalEstablish, before trusting any cross-validation score, whether provenance metadata even varies where it must.

MethodFor every VDJdb metadata field, count distinct levels within each epitope cohort; flag a field unusable if it is constant, or if its dominant level holds >95% of rows, in any private epitope. scripts/01_audit_metadata_viability.py.

ResultNot one field survives. vdjdb_score is constant 0 for LLWNGPMAV and LTDEMIAQY; method.verification is null for all 602 private rows; LTDEMIAQY is 97.8% a single reference.id and a single method.identification.

SettledA provenance feature can look strong in cross-validation on the public epitopes — where GILGFVFTL and NLVPMVATV span vdjdb_score 0–3 — and contribute literally zero on the private side. This became a standing rule: every new feature is checked for within-epitope variance on all three private epitopes before its LOEO score is believed.
E4

Metadata-only baseline: how much does evidence quality explain?

Rejected

GoalQuantify what VDJdb's own confidence score is worth.

MethodScore vdjdb_score directly, pooled and within epitope.

ResultPooled AUC 0.455 — below chance — and the direction flips between epitopes: 0.581 on GLCTLVAML, 0.399 on NLVPMVATV, 0.443 on YLQPRTFLL, undefined on TTDPSFLGRY (constant). The 158 score-3 NLVPMVATV entries validate at 15.8%, against 23.5% for score-0 in the same cohort.

SettledVDJdb's confidence score anti-correlates with functional validation. A model that learns "higher confidence means more reliable" on one epitope is actively harmed on the next. Metadata is kept as a documented ablation, never as a submission component.

E9

Cluster shape: real motif family or co-annotated artifact?

Rejected

GoalSeparate convergent recombination from a knot of near-duplicates that entered VDJdb together.

MethodPrompted by a diagnostic: NLVPMVATV's 23 high-density non-validating TCRs cluster tightly with each other (median cosine 0.148) but sit far from that epitope's validated TCRs (0.312), whereas elsewhere the high-density mistakes sit right on the positives (0.050, 0.088). So: measure cluster size, internal tightness, V/J purity, per-position CDR3 entropy, centrality.

ResultRestricted to rows inside a cluster, per-position CDR3β entropy scores 0.773 against density's 0.768, and internal tightness scores 0.275 — meaning tighter clusters validate less. Imputed across the full cohort, entropy collapses to 0.5553.

SettledA real signal with a clean mechanism — convergent recombination produces loose, diverse motif families, while a co-annotated knot is tight, and plain density scores both as "dense" — but it is largely a restatement of cluster membership and adds nothing on top.
E20

Four questions about what density is actually doing

Diagnostic

(a) Does the method lean on density more than on the labels?

It uses no labels at all. Density and structure are both unsupervised; all 1,782 labels serve only to validate. That is a strength — there is nothing to overfit, which is why the local estimate reproduced the leaderboard exactly — and an unresolved gap: 1,782 labels exist and the predictor extracts nothing from them. Every supervised route tried (E10, E13, E16) lost to variance or scored below chance.

(b) Are clustered TCRs merely label-pure, or genuinely positive-leaning?

45 clusters with ≥3 members, SCEPTR cosine ≤ 0.12

0% (pure negative)313

The 20–60% band is empty, and only 13 of 385 clustered TCRs (3.4%) sit in a pure-negative cluster. Cluster membership is positive evidence in its own right, not a statement about homogeneity. The ceiling is equally clear: only 487 of 1,782 TCRs are in any cluster, and about 54% of positives are singletons.

(c) Is anything available for singletons?

Density remains the best thing there — mean AUC 0.758 within singletons, against structure 0.722, multi-sample confidence 0.729, consistency 0.692, Pgen 0.497. One exception, and it is the weak epitope: on GLCTLVAML's 81 singletons density collapses to 0.551 while every structure-derived feature beats it (confidence 0.676, consistency 0.668, min-PAE 0.659).

(d) Near-neighbour label discordance

Some negatives are single-residue variants of validated positives, sitting inside otherwise-pure positive clusters: 34 of 872 negatives (3.9%), and relabelling them would move 0.8362 → 0.8602.

SettledTwo cautions raised in review, both correct. The relabelling arithmetic is selection-affected — these rows were picked because they sit close to positives, the same property that makes the model rank them high. And the discordance has two possible causes this analysis cannot separate: assay dropout, or genuine fine specificity. So 0.024 is not a demonstrated ceiling. The honest description is near-positive discordant clonotypes: negatives indistinguishable from validated positives by sequence, of unresolved cause. E22 later relocated their damage and E31 settled the cause.

(e) Why is GLCTLVAML weak, and does that threaten the private side?

Density fails there because everything is dense — 55.2% of its cohort has a neighbour inside the clustering radius, against 2.5–14.6% on the private three. None of the private epitopes share this pathology. (E21 later corrected the inference drawn from this: cohort geometry does not forecast AUC.)

Part threeRejected sequence approaches

E7

Generation probability

Rejected

GoalTest a neighbour-free alternative: are spurious annotations just typical repertoire draws?

MethodOLGA Pgen per chain and paired.

Result-log10 Pgen(paired) scores 0.417 — i.e. higher Pgen means more likely to validate, the opposite of the hypothesis. Not orthogonal either: Spearman with density reaches −0.63 on GILGFVFTL. Within singletons, 0.50–0.53.

SettledDense convergent clusters are built from easy-to-generate sequences, so Pgen mostly re-encodes density with an unstable sign.
E8

Background normalisation (TCRNET / ALICE-style)

Rejected

GoalSeparate antigen-driven convergence from "this region of TCR space is just crowded".

MethodDivide cohort density by density against the other epitopes' TCRs.

ResultOwn-cohort density 0.8396; difference 0.8357; log ratio 0.8262. Background density alone scores 0.60 positive.

SettledCrowded regions of TCR space are where genuinely public clonotypes live, so normalising subtracts signal rather than noise.
E10

Fitted fusion

Rejected

GoalAsk whether a learned combiner beats a fixed rank average.

MethodLogistic regression and gradient boosting over all density and cluster-shape features, LOEO.

ResultBest single unsupervised feature 0.8362; logistic regression on density only 0.8359; density + cluster shape 0.8299; depth-2 booster 0.8188; cluster shape alone 0.6846.

SettledFive folds cannot support a fitted combiner. Hard constraint adopted: any fusion is a zero-parameter rank average, and a component enters only if it adds > +0.02 in LOEO. This threshold governed every later decision.
E11

Graph refinement

Rejected

GoalTest whether global graph structure beats local degree.

MethodReplace degree centrality (plain density) with eigenvector centrality, PageRank, and 1–10 rounds of label propagation.

ResultDegree 0.8362; PageRank(0.85) 0.8329; propagation ×1 0.8297, ×3 0.8095, ×10 0.7417; eigenvector centrality 0.7486.

SettledMonotonically worse with more propagation. The signal is strictly local; eigenvector centrality collapses onto the largest cluster and stops ranking the others.
E13

Epitope-conditioned specificity model

Rejected

GoalPredict binding from TCR and peptide jointly, generalising to an unseen epitope.

MethodLightGBM over SCEPTR TCR embeddings concatenated with a BLOSUM62 peptide encoding, trained on 21,112 VDJdb paired pairs across 119 epitopes; negatives are the same TCRs paired with wrong epitopes so TCR identity carries no label. The target epitope is removed from training entirely — it must be, since the test TCRs are themselves VDJdb entries annotated to it.

ResultNLVPMVATV 0.553, GILGFVFTL 0.533, GLCTLVAML 0.531, TTDPSFLGRY 0.507, YLQPRTFLL 0.283. Mean 0.481 — below chance. Rank-averaged with density it drags 0.8415 down to 0.7293. Cost: 78 min wall, 1,153 CPU-min.

SettledPredicting binding to a peptide never seen in training remains unsolved. What the model learns is how generic a TCR looks against VDJdb, and on YLQPRTFLL the generic-looking TCRs are precisely the spurious annotations — the same failure shape as vdjdb_score in E4. Two independent failures with one mechanism: a quantity whose sign is not stable across epitopes is worse than no feature.
E16

Learning the metric that density lives in

Rejected

GoalReplace a hand-chosen bandwidth and isotropic distance with learned ones.

MethodLearn the kernel bandwidth alone (1 parameter) and a diagonal metric over the 64 SCEPTR dimensions (64 parameters, shrunk toward identity) by gradient descent on a within-epitope pairwise ranking surrogate. Strictly nested.

ResultPlain cosine σ=0.1 0.8396; scale-only 0.8199; diagonal 0.8144. Learned bandwidths came out at 0.20–0.26 in every fold, against a true optimum of 0.07.

Diagnosis, in three steps, because the first two were wrong

First guess — scale coupling. Standardising density within epitope before the loss did not help; σ still converged to 0.20–0.26. Second guess — loss shape. A bounded sigmoid was worse, and both surrogates are negatively correlated with AUC across a σ grid (−0.39 and −0.51). What is actually going on, from a temperature sweep:

temperatureloss argmin (σ)corr(−loss, AUC)
1.00.30−0.51
0.20.20−0.37
0.050.15−0.12
0.020.10+0.03

The surrogate only aligns with AUC as it stops being smooth — as temperature → 0 it becomes exactly the 1−AUC step function, by which point gradients have vanished.

SettledThe score distribution is heavy-tailed: a minority of TCRs sit in dense clusters at extreme density values. AUC gives full credit for ranking those at the very top; any smooth margin surrogate saturates there and is dominated by the ambiguous bulk. So the surrogate is least sensitive exactly where the metric gets most of its signal. This is a structural mismatch, not an implementation flaw. What the sweep did settle: per-epitope optimal bandwidth varies five-fold, 0.03 to 0.15.

E27

Pool expansion, audited and quantified

Rejected

GoalReplace E12's assertion with measurement.

(a) The query finds itself

Every test TCR came from VDJdb, so expanding with VDJdb risks counting the query as its own neighbour. 96.4% of queries find an exact self-hit, and the count is not constant — GILGFVFTL averages 2.19, so repeatedly curated clonotypes collect extra zero-distance "neighbours". Curation multiplicity tracks validation (1 record → 49.0% validation, 4+ records → 93.5%), making it a label proxy masquerading as density. Confirmed directly: raw expansion loses 0.0024 of mean AUC; query-removed expansion gains 0.0048.

(b) The aggregation operator decides whether expansion helps at all

operatorno poolpooleddelta

NLVPMVATV, whose pool is 94.8% a single dextramer study, goes −0.0354 under kernel-mean and +0.0131 under top-8: a mean over the whole pool is drowned by one study's records, while top-k never looks at them.

(c) What the private side can realise

Downsampling each public pool to the private epitopes' own distinct-clonotype multipliers, 30 repeats: +0.0013 on top-8, +0.0004 on the submitted blend. 88% of the public gain cannot transfer, because two of three private epitopes can add only ~4% more distinct clonotypes.

Three reasons not to quote the point value alone — all found by self-review after the first estimate was already written down:

  • It was first computed on a model not submitted. On the submitted configuration the macro estimate falls from +0.0013 to +0.0004, naive 95% interval [−0.0022, +0.0030], negative on two of three private epitopes.
  • The mean hides a 26-fold spread (GLCTLVAML +0.0158 to NLVPMVATV −0.0076, sd five times the mean). Averaging across public epitopes assumes the gain depends only on the multiplier — exactly the exchangeability E21 disproved. Five epitopes cannot estimate this quantity.
  • Random subsampling is optimistic. LTDEMIAQY's 5 available extras come from a pool that is 97.8% one reference; drawing only from each pool's dominant study gives −0.0002.
SettledNot "the gain is small" but "on our configuration there is no measurable gain". E12's verdict stands; its reasoning was incomplete — the problem is not only that few extra TCRs exist, but that the ones that do are duplicates from single studies.

E35

Reference-database version, source and leakage

Diagnostic

MethodThree VDJdb releases — 2024-06-13 (32,183 paired human rows), 2025-02-21 (35,672), 2026-06-03 (86,221) — with the pool measurement rerun on each.

Leakage audit, which had not been done and should have been. Searching every human reference for the Immunity 2026 DOI, the 2025 bioRxiv preprint, "TCRvdb", the Schumacher lab repo and "T-RAP" returns zero records each. The pool is independent of the labels.

E14

Structure prediction at scale

Adopted

GoalTest whether a predicted interface supplies signal orthogonal to sequence neighbourhood.

MethodFold all 2,636 TCR–peptide–MHC complexes with Boltz-2 — four chains: TCRα V, TCRβ V, allele-specific HLA α1α2, peptide; ~416 residues; single-sequence; 14 s each, 7.4 h total. Extract interface PAE and chain-pair ipTM.

SettledStructure confidence here largely reports how canonical the TCR is — typical sequences fold confidently everywhere through the shared pair representation — so it is not the orthogonal signal hoped for. E19

Docking consistency across diffusion samples

Rejected

GoalFind a structural quantity immune to the canonicality confound.

MethodRe-fold all 2,636 complexes with 5 diffusion samples (13.2 GPU-hours). Superpose each pair of samples on the MHC and peptide Cα, then take TCR Cα RMSD.

The hypothesis, and why it was wrong

Consistency was expected to be immune to the confound because all five samples share identical sequence input. That argument was wrong. The five samples share more than the input: they share the single already-computed trunk representation. The diffusion head samples a distribution conditioned on it, so a confident trunk yields a narrow conditional and therefore consistent samples. Consistency is downstream of trunk confidence.

ResultDocking RMSD spans 0.32–42.7 Å, so the dynamic range is real, but within-epitope Spearman against density is +0.530 — indistinguishable from the +0.528 of the confidence features it was meant to improve on. Blended, the best gain is +0.0010 against a +0.02 threshold.

Settled13.2 GPU-hours for +0.001. The pre-registered rule did its job. One thing worth keeping: consistency scores 0.775 on GLCTLVAML, the highest of any single feature on the project's weakest epitope — but weighting it heavily enough to exploit that drops the overall mean to 0.8128.

E25

Cross-check against an independent pipeline

Diagnostic

GoalA teammate ran a separate pipeline on the same competition. Recorded for what it confirms, what it exposes as a gap, and where the two disagree. Nothing from it was adopted into the submission.

The last two are the same population as our near-positive discordant clonotypes, but their frame explains why that population must exist and why no sequence method can reach it.
